Privacy Policy for ApexDoc: PDF Scanner & OCR

Last updated: 30 September 2026

ApexDoc ("the app"), operated by Swara Enterprises, is a document scanner and PDF tool. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your device, and if you choose to sign in with Google, copies are saved only in your own Google Drive.

Information the app handles

  • Documents and scans. PDFs you scan, import or open, and the images you pick from your gallery, are stored in the app's storage on your device. The app does not upload them to any server operated by us.
  • Recognized text (OCR). Text read from your documents is created on your device and saved in the app's private storage so you can search your documents. It is never sent to us.
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan documents. Photos are not sent to us.
  • Settings. Your preferences (text size, file naming, date format) are stored on your device.

Sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in is optional. The app works fully without it, including offline.

If you sign in:

  • Account details. The app receives your Google account name, email address and profile photo. They are used only to show your name and photo inside the app. They are kept on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Backup to your Google Drive. The app saves your PDFs and your app settings (for example text size and file naming) in a folder named "ApexDoc" in your own Google Drive. This uses the Google Drive permission "drive.file", which lets the app see and manage only the files and folders it created. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • No server of ours. Your files go directly from your device to your Google Drive. We do not receive, store or view them.
  • Mobile data. Backup runs on Wi-Fi by default. You can allow it on mobile data in the app; the app tells you when a backup is running on mobile data.
  • Free up space. The app may suggest removing the on-device copy of files that are already safely stored in your Drive. Nothing is removed unless you tap to confirm. Removed files stay in your document list, marked with a cloud icon, and are downloaded again from your Drive when you open them.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses Google user data only to provide the backup and restore features described above. It does not use it for advertising, does not sell it, and does not allow people at our end to read it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or our own analytics services and do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not run our own user accounts; sign-in uses your Google Account.

Third-party components

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive are provided by Google. Google's privacy policy applies to how Google handles your account and Drive data.
  • Google ML Kit (document scanning and text recognition) processes your documents on your device. ML Kit may send anonymous technical and performance data (such as error and usage statistics, never your document content) to Google, and some components are delivered and updated by Google Play services.
  • Sharing. When you choose to share or send a PDF (for example by email or WhatsApp), the file goes to the app you select. That app's own privacy policy applies.

Storage, deletion and your control

  • Documents and recognized text remain on your device until you delete them. Deleted documents stay in the Recycle Bin for 30 days and are then permanently removed. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.
  • Deleting a document in the app does not delete its backup copy in your Drive. To remove backups, delete the "ApexDoc" folder in your Google Drive.
  • To stop backup, sign out in the app. To withdraw the app's access completely, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove ApexDoc.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
